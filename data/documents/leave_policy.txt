Leave Policy

The organization recognizes the importance of maintaining a healthy work-life balance and provides employees with paid leave benefits to support personal, family, and health-related commitments.

Annual Leave Entitlement

All employees are entitled to 24 paid leave days per calendar year. These leave days may be utilized for vacation, personal matters, family responsibilities, medical appointments, or other approved reasons. Leave balances are credited and tracked according to the company's leave management system.

Advance Leave Requests

To ensure smooth business operations and adequate workload planning, employees are expected to submit leave requests at least three working days in advance of their intended absence whenever possible. Early submission allows managers and team members to make necessary arrangements and maintain continuity of work.

Emergency Leave

The company understands that unforeseen situations may arise. In cases of sudden illness, family emergencies, or other unexpected circumstances where advance notice is not possible, employees may request emergency leave. Employees should notify their reporting manager or designated authority as soon as reasonably possible and provide any required supporting information when requested.

Leave Approval Process

All planned leave requests must receive approval from the employee's reporting manager before the leave is taken. Employees are responsible for ensuring that critical tasks, project deadlines, and handovers are properly managed prior to their absence. Leave approval may be subject to business requirements and team resource availability.

Leave Carry Forward

Employees may be eligible to carry forward unused leave balances to the next calendar year in accordance with the company's leave carry-forward guidelines. Any maximum carry-forward limits, expiry dates, or encashment options will be governed by the applicable company policy in effect at that time.

Employee Responsibilities

While availing leave, employees are encouraged to:

Plan leave schedules responsibly.
Communicate absence details to relevant team members.
Complete or delegate critical tasks before going on leave.
Update out-of-office notifications when necessary.
Comply with all leave application procedures and policy requirements.
Management Rights

The company reserves the right to approve, defer, or decline leave requests based on operational needs, staffing requirements, business priorities, and compliance with company policies. Every effort will be made to accommodate employee leave requests while ensuring uninterrupted business operations.

Policy Compliance

Failure to follow the leave request and approval process may result in leave being recorded as unauthorized absence. Employees are encouraged to familiarize themselves with the complete leave policy and seek clarification from Human Resources whenever required.

The company is committed to supporting employee well-being while maintaining efficient business operations through fair and transparent leave management practices.
