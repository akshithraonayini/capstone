Human Resources (HR) Policy
1. Professional Conduct and Workplace Behavior

All employees are expected to maintain the highest standards of professionalism, integrity, and ethical conduct while representing the organization. Employees should treat colleagues, customers, vendors, and stakeholders with respect, courtesy, and fairness at all times. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, or any behavior that negatively impacts the workplace environment will not be tolerated.

Employees are expected to:

Demonstrate honesty and accountability in all work-related activities.
Foster a collaborative and inclusive work culture.
Respect diversity and individual differences.
Comply with company policies, procedures, and applicable laws.
Use company resources responsibly and for legitimate business purposes.

Professional conduct standards apply equally to employees working from the office, remotely, or in hybrid work arrangements.

2. Working Hours and Attendance

The standard working hours for employees are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with designated breaks as applicable under company guidelines.

Employees are expected to:

Be available and productive during scheduled working hours.
Attend meetings, training sessions, and other official activities as required.
Maintain accurate attendance records where applicable.
Inform their manager promptly in case of delays or unforeseen circumstances affecting attendance.

Flexible work arrangements may be available based on business requirements and management approval. Employees should adhere to team schedules and ensure their availability for collaboration during agreed core working hours.

3. Leave and Absence Management

Employees must communicate planned absences, vacations, medical appointments, or personal leave requests to their reporting manager well in advance. Leave requests should be submitted through the designated company system and approved before the absence begins whenever possible.

In cases of emergency or unplanned absences:

Employees must notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.
Supporting documentation may be required for extended absences.
Employees should provide updates regarding their expected return-to-work date when applicable.

Failure to properly communicate absences may result in attendance-related disciplinary action.

4. Remote Work Expectations

Employees working remotely are expected to maintain the same standards of performance, professionalism, and accountability as office-based employees.

Remote employees should:

Ensure a reliable internet connection and suitable work environment.
Remain reachable through approved communication channels during working hours.
Attend virtual meetings punctually and actively participate.
Protect company information from unauthorized access within their remote workspace.
Follow all company policies regarding confidential information and technology usage.
5. Security and Information Protection

Employees are required to comply with all company security, confidentiality, privacy, and data protection policies. Protecting company assets, customer information, and sensitive business data is a critical responsibility for every employee.

Employees must:

Use strong passwords and approved authentication methods.
Maintain confidentiality of proprietary information.
Report security incidents, suspicious activities, or data breaches immediately.
Follow established procedures for storing, transmitting, and disposing of sensitive information.
Use only authorized software, devices, and communication platforms for company business.

Violations of security policies may result in disciplinary action, including termination of employment where appropriate.

6. Use of Company Assets

Company equipment, software, systems, networks, and other resources are provided primarily for business purposes. Employees are responsible for maintaining company property and using it responsibly.

Employees should:

Keep devices secure and protected from theft or damage.
Avoid installing unauthorized applications or software.
Report lost, stolen, or damaged equipment immediately.
Return all company property upon separation from employment.
7. Health, Safety, and Well-being

The organization is committed to providing a safe, healthy, and supportive working environment. Employees are expected to follow all workplace health and safety procedures and promptly report hazards, accidents, or unsafe conditions.

Employees should:

Follow emergency procedures and safety guidelines.
Participate in mandatory safety training.
Support initiatives promoting employee wellness and mental health.
Take reasonable precautions to protect themselves and others while performing work-related duties.
8. Performance and Accountability

Employees are expected to perform their duties to the best of their abilities and contribute positively to organizational objectives. Regular performance evaluations may be conducted to assess employee achievements, development needs, and overall contributions.

Employees are encouraged to:

Set clear goals aligned with business objectives.
Seek continuous learning and professional development opportunities.
Accept constructive feedback and work toward improvement.
Collaborate effectively with colleagues and teams.
9. Policy Compliance

All employees are required to read, understand, and comply with this HR Policy and any related company policies. Violations may result in corrective action, disciplinary measures, or other consequences deemed appropriate by the organization.

The company reserves the right to modify, update, or revise its HR policies at any time to address business requirements, legal obligations, or organizational changes. Employees will be informed of significant policy updates through official communication channels.

Acknowledgment

By accepting employment and continuing to work with the organization, employees acknowledge their responsibility to comply with all applicable company policies, procedures, and professional standards outlined in this HR Policy.
